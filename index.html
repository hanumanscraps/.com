Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum quantity for a free pickup?
For residential, we recommend a minimum of 50kg. For commercial and industrial scrap, we provide free pickup for 100kg and above.
How are your scrap prices determined?
Our rates are dynamically updated based on the current market industrial rates for metals, paper, and plastics. We ensure you get the most competitive value.
Do you accept E-waste?
Yes, we safely collect and recycle e-waste including old computers, batteries, wires, and appliances.
